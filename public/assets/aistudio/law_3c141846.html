Our Core Foundation

Why YEP Exists

Talent is universally distributed, but opportunity is not. We provide safe spaces, rigorous academic support, and career readiness to help youth break cycles of poverty and thrive.

📚

Academic Excellence

Free one-on-one after-school tutoring, college prep workshops, SAT/ACT assistance, and direct application mentorship to ensure higher education is within reach.

💼

Workforce & Apprenticeship

Hands-on technical certifications, resume polish, mock interviews, and subsidized corporate apprenticeships connecting young talent directly to living-wage careers.

🌱

Leadership & Civic Voice

Empowering youth to design community service projects, speak at municipal policy forums, and champion neighborhood-led environmental and social initiatives.

Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Who is eligible to participate in YEP programs?

+

Our programs are open to youth aged 12 to 24 from underserved and underrepresented communities. All regular academic, tech, and creative workshops are 100% free of charge.

How do I become a volunteer or mentor?

+

Click the "Volunteer" button to fill out our quick intake form. We require a background check and a short orientation session before matching mentors with students.

Where do my donations go?

+

Over 87 cents of every dollar goes directly into youth programming, including laptops for students, certified educators, meals during afterschool sessions, and college micro-scholarships.

Can companies partner with YEP for corporate social responsibility?

+

Yes! We partner with leading corporations for sponsored apprenticeships, employee volunteer days, guest speaker panels, and technology donations.

01

UML · Use case diagram

Who uses YEP and what they can do

Every role shares account, profile, and messaging tools, with additional actions based on its responsibilities.

Youth participant

  • Register and manage profile
  • Enrol in a Level 1 exchange
  • Join a mixed Level 2 team
  • Develop a project and submit assessments
  • View team, points, and certificate

Mentor

  • Advise teams
  • Review impact projects
  • Communicate with participants

Facilitator

  • Create exchange sessions
  • Form balanced teams by education level
  • Set and grade assessments
  • Moderate content

Administrator

  • Manage users and roles
  • Issue certificates
  • View platform reports

Employer and partner opportunity posting is shown as future scope in the source diagram.

03

Database · Entity relationship diagram

How YEP’s data is organised

Primary keys identify records; foreign keys connect related records.

USER

PK user_id · name, email, role, country, age, education_level, points · FK team_id

TEAM

PK team_id · FK mentor_id · team_name, created_at

IMPACT_PROJECT

PK project_id · FK team_id · title, theme, status

ASSESSMENT

PK assess_id · FK user_id, evaluator_id · score, feedback, marks, status

ACTIVITY

PK activity_id · title, level_type, start_date, max_spots

ENROLMENT

PK enrolment_id · FK user_id, activity_id · enrol_date, status

MESSAGE

PK msg_id · FK sender_id, receiver_id · body, sent_at, is_read

CERTIFICATE

PK cert_id · FK user_id, issuer_id · issue_date, cert_type

Core links: users enrol in activities, belong to teams, submit assessments, exchange messages, and receive certificates. Teams develop impact projects.

Mentor

Facilitator

Sends sessions, assessments, marks, feedback, and team-building requests.

Administrator
